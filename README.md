# Fresh Market Hab — Enterprise BI Solution

**Academic case study · Business analysis · Power BI · Qlik Sense · BI architecture**

A business intelligence project examining how a retail organisation with fragmented operational systems could move towards self-service, decision-ready reporting. The work includes **working dashboard prototypes** in Power BI and Qlik Sense, a comparative technology evaluation, and a **proposed** end-to-end analytics architecture.

🌐 **[Read the full portfolio case study](https://chetna-1211.github.io/#case-fmh)**

## Business problem

Fresh Market Hab (FMH), a case-study retail organisation, operates data across separate sales, inventory, CRM, HR and other systems. This limits a unified view of performance and increases reliance on manually requested reports. The objective was to recommend an integrated BI approach that supports reliable, accessible reporting.

## What I worked on

- Developed **two Power BI dashboard pages** and a comparable **Qlik Sense executive overview** with interactive filters.
- Analysed sample KPIs including revenue, gross profit, profitability, units sold, sales channel, and operational performance.
- Evaluated **Power BI, Qlik Sense, Tableau and Looker** against weighted criteria, including interoperability, usability, governance, scalability and implementation effort.
- Proposed a solution combining **Microsoft Fabric Data Factory → Snowflake → semantic model → Power BI**; the proposed enterprise pipeline was **not implemented**.

## Technology evaluation and recommendation

Power BI ranked first for this *specific* case-study scenario in a weighted assessment: **4.75/5**, followed by Tableau **4.45/5**, Qlik Sense **4.25/5** and Looker **4.05/5**. The scores are project judgments, not universal market rankings. Power BI was recommended based on practical dashboard usability, reporting needs and compatibility with the proposed Microsoft data-integration layer.
